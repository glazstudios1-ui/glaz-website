How we work with you

What happens around the photos, so the day itself feels easy.

  1. A week before

    We plan it with you

    You fill in your timings, places and the people that matter. We call you to go over everything, and every member of our crew gets the plan.

  2. The day

    We arrive before you’re ready

    Your crew knows the schedule, the venue and who to look for. You don’t have to direct anyone.

  3. 5 days after

    Your highlight

    A short edit of photos and video, ready to share while everyone is still talking about the night.

  4. 25 days after

    Everything else

    Your full gallery and films, delivered in a private online gallery you can share with family.
