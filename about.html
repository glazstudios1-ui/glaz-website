About usGlaz Studios

Glaz means eye.

A wedding passes in one day. We are the eye that keeps it.

Glaz Studios is a photography and film studio in Cairo. We photograph and film weddings as one team, from the first hour of getting ready to the last song, and we keep what really happened: the people, the light and the feeling of the day.

GLAZ STUDIOS

Photography

Our photographers work as one team on your day, and every photo is edited by hand in one look, so your whole gallery feels like one story.

GLAZ STUDIOS

Film

Our cinematographers work side by side with the photographers, from the same plan, so your film and your photos tell the same day.

01What we believe

How we work

  1. Real, not staged

    We don’t pose you or ask you to do it again. We catch the moments as they come: the tears, the laughs and the dance floor.

  2. The whole day, start to end

    From getting ready to the last song, we follow the day as one story, so nothing in between is lost.

  3. One team, one plan

    You write your day once on your private link. Every photographer and cinematographer works from the same plan.

Everything your day needs

  • Media coveragePhotos and clips for your pages while the night is still going.
